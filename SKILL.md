# Yan Er Ding Brand Design

Create work that is recognizably 言二丁: deep burgundy as the field, champagne gold as the imprint, Chinese serif type as the structure, and product photography as the main source of full color. The result should feel like a small luxury shoe boutique, editorial lookbook, or bespoke workshop—not a generic ecommerce promotion or SaaS template.

## Route the task

1. Read [references/brand-system.md](references/brand-system.md) for every task.
2. For websites, apps, or coded prototypes, also read [references/digital-implementation.md](references/digital-implementation.md).
3. For posters, packaging, presentations, contracts, or other fixed-layout material, also read [references/print-and-copy.md](references/print-and-copy.md).
4. Inspect the target before changing it. Preserve required content, behavior, information architecture, and framework conventions unless the user asks to change them.

## Work with the system

- Classify the surface before designing: customer-facing, staff/admin, or fixed-layout/print. Let the relevant reference tune density and hierarchy.
- Use the exact tokens and real logo files supplied in `assets/`. Copy only the assets needed by the target; do not make the target depend on this skill's installation path.
- Treat the logos as locked artwork. Do not redraw, stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, or decorate them.
- Prefer the user's authorized product photography. This repository intentionally does not ship the private product catalog. If suitable imagery is unavailable, keep a clearly marked image slot or ask for assets; do not invent products, prices, materials, inventory, or brand claims.
- For a new surface with no supplied information architecture or routes, keep navigation and destinations minimal and visibly provisional. Do not fabricate business sections, catalog structure, or live URLs.
- Preserve the brand's two-hue discipline. Semantic success, warning, danger, and info colors are allowed only where the meaning would otherwise be unclear.
- Keep copy quiet, specific, and editorial. Do not introduce sales shouting, emoji, unsupported numbers, or exclamation-heavy slogans.
- Do not publish, deploy, purchase, or replace production assets unless the user explicitly requests that external action.

## Verify the result

Before handing off:

- Confirm the target still works and all required information remains present.
- Check the real rendered output at its intended size. For digital work, cover desktop and mobile plus keyboard focus and reduced motion. For fixed layouts, inspect the exported artifact at final dimensions.
- Check logo clarity and safe space, Chinese serif typography, burgundy/gold hierarchy, image aspect ratios, text overflow, contrast, and absence of the prohibited patterns in the brand reference.
- Report what changed, which source assets were used, and what was actually verified. Distinguish a local preview from a deployed or printed result.
